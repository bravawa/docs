Every campaign that has started sending has a results page. Open it by clicking the campaign in **Campaigns › All campaigns** (or **Sent**, **Sending**), or right-click the row and choose **View report**.

While a campaign is still sending, the page refreshes itself every 10 seconds and the status pill moves from *Sending · 40%* to *Sent*. A scheduled campaign opens the same page with the note *This campaign hasn't started sending yet. Its numbers show up here once it does.*

<Note>
A draft has no results. Clicking a draft opens it in the editor instead.
</Note>

## The header

Under the campaign name and status is one line saying when it went, which lists it went to, and the number it was sent from. The buttons on the right:

| Button | What it does | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Export** | Downloads a CSV file with every recipient and how it went, including the times it was delivered, clicked, replied and opted out. | Managers and Admins |
| **Resend to people who didn't get it (N)** | Opens **Resend this campaign?** See below. | Managers and Admins |
| **Stop sending** | While it's sending: asks **Stop sending "…"?** Texts already sent can't be taken back; credits for the rest come back to you. | Managers and Admins |
| **Cancel schedule** | While it's scheduled: turns it back into a draft. No credits are taken. | Managers and Admins |
| **Duplicate** | Copies the campaign as a new draft. | Everyone |

## How it went

A row of cards shows each step, with a bar for its share of the people texted:

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent to** | Everyone the campaign was sent to. |
| **Delivered** | Texts the carrier confirmed, with the percentage and how many were *not delivered*. |
| **Clicked the link** | People who tapped their `{Booking link}` and *opened the booking page*. Shows only when the text had a booking link. |
| **Replied** | People who texted back. Two-way texting only; replies are *in Communications*. |
| **Opted out** | People who replied STOP or (outgoing only) used their opt-out link after this campaign. |

## Recipients

The **Recipients** table lists each person with **Person**, **Status**, **Detail** and **Time**. Use the tabs to filter: **All**, **Replied**, **Clicked**, **Not delivered** and **Opted out**, each with its count. Click **Show more** for the next page.

| Status | Detail you may see |
| --- | --- |
| **Delivered** | — |
| **Replied** | The start of their reply in quotes, or *Replied · see Communications*. |
| **Clicked link** | *Opened booking page* |
| **Not delivered** | *Landline*, *Number not in service*, *Carrier blocked* or *Carrier error* |
| **Opted out** | *Replied STOP*, *Used opt-out link* or *Opted out by the pharmacy* |
| **Waiting** | Still in the queue (while sending). |
| **Left out** | *Opted out before their turn*, *Consent ended before their turn*, *Landline*, *Same number twice* or *Campaign stopped first* |

## The side panel

- **The text**: the message exactly as it went out, and what it cost: *N texts × $0.060*, **Sending from your own number** if used, **Back for texts that never left** (refunds), and **Taken from credits**. If the $6.00 minimum was charged, it says so.
- **Latest replies** (two-way only): the newest replies. Click one to open that conversation in **Comms**, or click **Open in Communications** for all text conversations.
- **Replies and clicks by hour** (or **Link clicks by hour** for outgoing only): a small bar chart of when people responded.
- **Why N didn't get it**: failed texts by reason (**Landline — can't take texts**, **Number not in service**, **Carrier blocked it**, **Other carrier error**) and people left out before their turn (**Opted out before their turn**, **Consent ran out**, **Known landline**, **Stopped before sending**). Landlines found this way are marked so they're skipped next time.

With outgoing-only texting, a note explains that the pharmacy doesn't get replies: people opt out with their own link, and the booking link is how you see who's interested.

## Resend to the people it missed

After a campaign has finished, a Manager or Admin can send it once more to the people whose text failed, other than landlines, and who can still be texted today.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the resend">
    Click **Resend to people who didn't get it (N)**. The window works out the price first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the price">
    It asks, for example, *Resend to 13 people who didn't get it? $6.00 comes out of your credits (the $6.00 minimum applies).* A resend is priced like any campaign, so the minimum and the own-number fee apply again. If you're short, click **Buy credits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend">
    Click **Resend**, or **Not now**. The resend is a new campaign named *… · resend*, and it sends right away, inside quiet hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once resent, the button becomes **See the resend**, and the resend's page links back with **See the original campaign**. When the button is grey, hover over it to see why: *Still sending*, *Already resent*, *This is already a resend*, *Stopped campaigns can't be resent*, *Everyone got it* or *Nobody to resend to*.
