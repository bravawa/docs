Everyone at your pharmacy has one **role**. It's shown under their name on **Who's working?** and set by a Manager or Admin in **Settings › Manage employees** (see [Manage employees](/settings/manage-employees)). Your role decides what you can change; everyone can read and work in the modules your pharmacy has.

## The five roles

| Role | In short |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Runs the pharmacy's account. Everything a Manager can do, plus adding, changing and removing other Admins. |
| **Manager** | Runs the pharmacy day to day: the team, the plan and billing, and pharmacy-wide settings. Signs as a pharmacist. |
| **Pharmacist** | Signs and approves clinical work: master formulas, compounding records, narcotic reconciliations, pharmacist sign-offs on forms. |
| **Technician** | Does the daily work in every module, without the pharmacist sign-offs. |
| **Assistant** | Front-counter staff, with the same standing as a technician. |

<Info>
  Managers and Admins count as pharmacists wherever a pharmacist signs (compounding, narcotics, forms), and their signature reads "Pharmacist". Blister packs is the exception: see the table below.
</Info>

### The top role

Some sign-in settings belong to the pharmacy's **top role**: its Admins, or its Managers when the pharmacy has no Admin. Only the top role can:

- Turn two-step sign-in on or off.
- Change the pharmacy's sign-in email or password.

See [Sign-in security](/settings/sign-in-security).

### Blister packs

Blister packs looks at each person's licence, not only their role. A Manager or Admin can tick **Licensed pharmacist** or **Registered technician** for each person in **Settings › Manage employees**, and each person adds their licence number in **Settings › Profile**.

| Check | Who can sign it |
| --- | --- |
| **Clinical verification** | A Pharmacist (or anyone with the **Licensed pharmacist** tick) who has a licence number. |
| **Product verification** | Anyone who can sign the clinical verification, or anyone with the **Registered technician** tick and a licence number. |

Managers and Admins sign blister checks only when one of the ticks is set. Assistants and unticked technicians can't sign either check. Changing Blister packs settings is for Managers and Admins.
