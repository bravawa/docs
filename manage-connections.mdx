<Info>
  This guide is for **central fill pharmacies**. Connections must be established before a dispensing pharmacy can send you central fill orders.
</Info>

## Overview

The **Connections** page is a two-panel interface. The left panel shows all pharmacies available to connect with, and the right panel shows your currently connected pharmacies.

Navigate to **Compounding → Central Fill → Manage Connections**.

---

## Adding a Connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the pharmacy">
    Browse the **Select Pharmacies to Connect** panel on the left, or use the search bar to find a specific pharmacy by name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the pharmacy">
    Click on a pharmacy to select it. You can select multiple pharmacies at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect Selected Pharmacies**. The selected pharmacies will immediately appear in the **Connected Pharmacies** panel on the right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can connect multiple pharmacies in a single action — select as many as needed before clicking **Connect Selected Pharmacies**.
</Tip>

---

## Removing a Connection

In the **Connected Pharmacies** panel on the right, click **Remove** next to the pharmacy you want to disconnect.

<Warning>
  Removing a connection will prevent that dispensing pharmacy from sending you new orders. Any orders already submitted will not be affected.
</Warning>
